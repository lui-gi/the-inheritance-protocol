# House Rule: AI Is a Guide, Not the Investigator

One last rule before Blackthorn House opens its doors.

## What is allowed

You may use an AI assistant to **learn**: how a tool works, what a command does, what a file format is, or what a concept like hashing or SQL injection means. Use it the way you would use a tutorial or a search engine.

## What is not allowed

You must not hand the challenge files, task text, or questions to an AI and ask it to find the flags or name the killer. Do not paste logs, images, code, or web pages from this room into an AI to have it solve them for you.

## Why

Every question in this room can be answered with the toolkit from the first task, and the whole point is to do the forensics yourself. The thrill of catching a killer is a lot smaller when a chatbot caught them for you. Organizers may ask how you found a flag, so be ready to explain your steps.
