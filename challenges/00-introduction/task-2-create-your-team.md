# Assemble Your Investigators

No detective works alone, and nobody wants to be the only person in the room explaining hash functions to a portrait. Before you open a single file, form your team.

## Building your team

- Find your teammates. An organizer will tell you the team size and help anyone who has not found a group yet.
- Pick a **team name**. Spooky, punny, or dramatic are all encouraged. "The Usual Suspects" is taken by everyone, every year, so be original.
- Every teammate should join this room on their own TryHackMe account, so everyone can download the files and submit answers.
- Give your team name to an organizer so they can track your progress.

## Working together

- **Split up, then share.** Different people can dig into different files, but share everything you find. Clues from early tasks unlock later ones.
- **Keep one shared notebook.** A shared doc, a group chat, or a sheet of paper in the middle of the table all work. Record every name, device ID, date, and hash.
- **Everyone types.** Take turns at the keyboard. The person who has never opened a terminal before should get a turn too.
- **Argue about suspects, not about each other.** It is Halloween, the host is dead, and that is drama enough.
