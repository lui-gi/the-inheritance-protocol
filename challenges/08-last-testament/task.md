# The Last Testament

Over dessert, Adrian Vale told his family: "By midnight, none of you will own what you think you own." He did not make it to midnight. But Adrian never trusted a plan that depended on him being in the room, and the estate archive was holding something of his with a release time on it: 00:00, 1 November.

You have spent seven tasks collecting evidence. Now put it in order.

This task has no download. The investigation's case board is online at:

- https://blackthorn-case-board.vercel.app/

Open it in your browser. The board remembers your progress in this browser, so stay in the same browser (and not a private window) until you finish.

The board shows the night as a timeline of cards, joined in order by red string. Four of them are exhibits still missing their evidence. Each one asks for a single piece of evidence you already found, and tells you which task it came from. Pin all four and the sealed envelope at the end opens: the message Adrian recorded the night before he died.

The archive will not simply play it for you. It was told to release that message to one role only, and you are not that role. Fortunately the release page was built by the Legal Office, in the same spirit as their case portal.

A few pointers:

- Go back to your notes, or to the earlier tasks, for the four answers. Paste them exactly. Capitals do not matter.
- Read what each exhibit says once it is pinned. Together the four exhibits are the whole case.
- When the archive refuses you, read the refusal carefully. It tells you which role it wants, which role it thinks you are, and where it got that idea.
- A website can ask your browser to remember things in cookies. Your browser will also let you read those cookies, and change them. Press F12 to open the developer tools, then look for Cookies under the Application tab (Chrome, Edge) or the Storage tab (Firefox). Double-click a value to edit it, then reload the page.
- A site that asks the visitor who they are, and believes the answer, has not checked anything.
